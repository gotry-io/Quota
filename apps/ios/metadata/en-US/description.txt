Quota shows how much coding-agent quota you have left, on the phone that is in your pocket.

Sign in to Codex, Claude Code, or Grok right on this iPhone and Quota reads your remaining quota from the provider itself. Add a Quota Account and your Macs running QuotaBar report their quota and Usage into it too; Overview is both, merged into one row per subscription.

WHAT YOU SEE

• Remaining quota for every subscription, with reset times and a pace line that says whether you will run out before the reset
• Today Usage: tokens and API-equivalent cost, with a daily breakdown, model share, cache hit rate, and a weekday-by-hour rhythm
• Your Devices, marked Active, Idle, or Not reporting
• Provider status, so an outage reads as the provider's problem rather than yours
• Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, in three sizes
• Optional notifications when remaining quota crosses a threshold you set, when a window resets, or when your pace says you will run out

WHAT STAYS ON THIS IPHONE

A provider sign-in is the provider's own page, shown in the app. Quota keeps only the sign-in cookie it leaves, in this iPhone's Keychain, and sends it only to that provider. It is never uploaded to Quota, and Remove deletes it.

What Quota uploads, when you are signed in to a Quota Account, is the numbers: remaining quota, reset times, and token totals. Never:

• Provider credentials, cookies, or API keys
• Prompts, completions, tool payloads, or raw events
• Local file paths, session IDs, or conversation IDs

PROVIDERS AND AGENTS

On this iPhone: Codex, Claude Code, and Grok.

From a Mac running QuotaBar: Codex, Claude Code, Grok, OpenRouter, DeepSeek, Kimi Code, LiteLLM, Cursor, Gemini CLI, GitHub Copilot, Antigravity, and OpenCode Go quota, and Usage analytics for Codex, Claude Code, Grok, OpenCode, Pi, Cursor, Gemini CLI, GitHub Copilot, Kilo, and Antigravity.

QUOTA ACCOUNT

A Quota Account is optional. Without one, the tabs still open on whatever this phone read for itself. With one, every device you sign in reports into the same place, and the website shows the same Overview. Sign in with Apple, with GitHub, or with an emailed link; one Account can hold more than one way in.

ACCOUNT DELETION

Delete the Account from Settings › Delete Account. That continues on the website after you sign in again, and removes the Account, its Devices, and everything they reported.

Requires iOS 17 or later. QuotaBar for Mac is free and open source at https://quota.gotry.io
