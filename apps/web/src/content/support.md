# Support

## Notifications

Remaining-quota alerts and reset reminders are configured and evaluated in QuotaBar on your Mac. The
website does not send notifications.

## FAQ

### Where does the data come from?

QuotaBar reads provider quota and agent logs on your Mac. Credentials, prompts, paths, and session
IDs stay there.

Quota collection supports Codex, Claude Code, Grok, OpenRouter, DeepSeek, Kimi Code, LiteLLM, and
Cursor. Local Usage analytics supports Codex, Claude Code, Grok, OpenCode, Pi, and Cursor logs.

If you sign in, QuotaBar uploads only the numbers: remaining quota and privacy-preserving Usage
totals. The website reads that Account.

### When is Quota for iPhone available?

Quota for iPhone is coming soon. When it ships, it will read the Account reported by QuotaBar.

### How do I send a diagnostic report?

In QuotaBar: Settings › Support › Diagnostics › Copy Report. Paste the copied report into a GitHub
issue or an email. The report is the service-owned diagnostic text; it does not include credentials,
prompts, paths, or session IDs.

### How do I delete my account?

Sign in on the website and open [Account](/my). From there you can delete a Device or the Account.
Deleting a Device removes that Device and its Quota and Usage data. Deleting the Account removes
the Account.
