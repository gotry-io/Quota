Welcome to Quota 1.0.

Quota is the iPhone companion to QuotaBar. Connect the GitHub Account you already use on a Mac to see remaining coding-agent quota, Today Usage, and which collection Devices are Active, Idle, or Not reporting.

Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets keep remaining quota in glance range. Optional notifications can remind you when remaining quota crosses a threshold you set.

This iPhone is a read-only viewer. Collection, credentials, and agent logs stay on the Mac.
